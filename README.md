# Privacy Policy — Doctor Finder – Find Doctors

**Effective Date:** November 12, 2026  
**Developer:** IARSoft  
**Application:** Doctor Finder – Find Doctors

This Privacy Policy explains how **Doctor Finder – Find Doctors**, operated by **IARSoft**, collects, uses, shares, stores, and deletes information when patients and doctors use the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect or process the following information:

### 1.1 Account Information
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Sign-in provider
- Account ID
- Account type (Patient or Doctor)

### 1.2 Profile Information
- Age or date of birth
- Address or location you enter
- Profile photo
- Other information you voluntarily provide

### 1.3 Doctor Information
- Medical specialty
- Qualifications
- Professional experience
- Clinic or practice details
- Availability and schedule
- Consultation fees
- Profile photo
- Articles submitted by doctors

### 1.4 Appointment Information
- Appointment requests and history
- Selected doctor
- Appointment date and time
- Patient name
- Contact number
- Patient type
- Appointment status

### 1.5 Chat Messages and Files
- Messages exchanged through the application
- Images you choose to send in conversations

### 1.6 Medical Records
- Medical files you voluntarily upload
- File titles and categories

### 1.7 App and Notification Information
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device token, if notifications are enabled
- Technical information processed by our service providers to provide, maintain, and secure their services

**Location Information:** The application does not collect precise device location. Any address or location included in your profile is information you manually provide.

**Photo and File Access:** The application accesses only the photos and files you select for profile images, chats, articles, or medical records.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing user accounts.
- Authenticating and securing accounts.
- Managing patient and doctor profiles.
- Reviewing doctor registration applications.
- Displaying doctor profiles, schedules, and articles.
- Managing appointment requests and bookings.
- Providing chat and saved-doctor features.
- Storing medical records that you choose to upload.
- Sending requested notifications.
- Responding to customer support inquiries.
- Handling privacy requests and account deletion requests.

**We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.**

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We share information only when necessary to provide and operate the application's services.

### 3.1 Patients and Doctors
- Appointment information is shared between the patient and the selected doctor.
- Chat messages are displayed to the participants in the conversation.
- Doctor profiles, schedules, and published articles may be visible to application users.

### 3.2 Application Administrators

Authorized administrators may access information when necessary to:

- Operate and maintain the application.
- Review doctor registration applications.
- Provide technical support.
- Investigate security or service-related issues.

### 3.3 Third-Party Service Providers

Doctor Finder – Find Doctors uses the following Google services:

**Google Firebase**
- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Firebase Storage
- Firebase Cloud Messaging

These services help us provide authentication, database storage, file storage, and push notifications.

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google processes your authentication request.

You can review their privacy practices:

- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Data Storage and Security

Account information and uploaded content are stored using Google Firebase services.

We implement authentication and configured access controls intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential. Please contact us if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

## 5. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account information while your account remains active and as necessary to provide the application's services.

You can request permanent account deletion using either of the following methods.

### 5.1 Delete Your Account Through the App

1. Open Doctor Finder – Find Doctors.
2. Navigate to **Settings**.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
4. Follow the identity verification and confirmation steps.

### 5.2 Request Account Deletion Through the Website

You can find the complete account deletion instructions in [Section 10 — Account Deletion](#10-account-deletion--doctor-finder--find-doctors).

If you cannot access your account, contact us at:

**Email:** [iarsoftofficial@gmail.com](mailto:iarsoftofficial@gmail.com)

Please send your request from the email address associated with your account.

### 5.3 What Happens After Account Deletion?

After receiving and verifying a deletion request, we delete the Firebase account and associated application data and files, including:

- User profile information
- Appointment records
- Chat messages
- Saved doctors
- Uploaded medical records
- Doctor articles

Information that must be retained to comply with applicable legal obligations may be kept for the required period.

Provider backups and security logs may remain temporarily according to the relevant provider's retention practices.

## 10. Account Deletion — Doctor Finder – Find Doctors

Doctor Finder – Find Doctors provides users with the ability to request permanent deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

### 10.1 How to Delete Your Account Through the App

Follow these steps:

1. Open the Doctor Finder – Find Doctors application.
2. Navigate to **Settings**.
3. Select **Delete Account**.
4. Complete the identity verification process.
5. Confirm your account deletion request.

### 10.2 Request Account Deletion Through Email

If you cannot access your account or use the in-app deletion option, you can request account deletion by emailing:

**Email:** [iarsoftofficial@gmail.com](mailto:iarsoftofficial@gmail.com)

Please include:

- Your registered email address.
- Your account type (Patient or Doctor).
- A clear statement requesting permanent account deletion.

For security purposes, we may request additional information to verify account ownership before processing your request.

### 10.3 Information Deleted

After verifying and processing your deletion request, we delete the account and associated application information, including:

- Account credentials and authentication account.
- Personal profile information.
- Patient and doctor profile records.
- Appointment records.
- Chat messages and associated files.
- Saved doctors.
- Uploaded medical records.
- Doctor articles and related content.

### 10.4 Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Temporary backups and security logs may remain according to the retention practices of our service providers.
