Delete Your Account

Doctor Finder – Find Doctors

Users of Doctor Finder – Find Doctors can request permanent deletion of their accounts and associated personal information.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open Doctor Finder – Find Doctors.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Complete the confirmation process.

Request Deletion by Email

If you cannot access your account, email us at:

iarsoftofficial@gmail.com

Use the email address associated with your account.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Account and profile information
  • Appointment records
  • Chat messages
  • Saved doctors
  • Uploaded medical records
  • Doctor articles

Data Retention

Information required by law may be retained for the legally required period. Provider backups and security logs may remain temporarily under provider retention policies.
